Claudia Chircu

Mapping complex software systems, deep space coordinates, and personal running tracks

Hi! I'm Clau, and my world revolves around capturing details—whether I'm writing code, documenting software, or tracking stars.😄

I built this website to bring those pieces together, using the HTML and CSS skills I sharpen every day in my work as a Technical Writer. In my role, turning complex technology into clear, user-friendly experiences is an art form. I spend my days structuring everything from comprehensive Admin and Quick Reference PDF Guides to interactive Online Help portals, FAQs, and Release Notes. To make these assets as engaging as possible, I build my documentation using MadCap Flare, manage knowledge bases in ServiceNow, and use Camtasia to edit short, visual feature videos that show users exactly how a new deployment works.

That exact same obsession with precision and process naturally bleeds into my life outside the office. When I look away from my text editor, I simply change my lens—turning my attention to tracking light data to map deep-space nebulas through astrophotography, calculating complex routes in strategic board games, or measuring my pace in local running competitions to fundraise for local charities. To me, whether it is a line of code, a technical manual, or a running track, it's all about finding the perfect path.🤩
