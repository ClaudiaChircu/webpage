The Mobile and Data solutions provide companies and employees flexible working options, like bundles and devices. Internet of Things (IoT) solutions connect physical devices and assets to a global network, enabling businesses to gather real-time data, automate processes, and drive smart automation. This refined my ability to translate technical telemetry, data-routing protocols, and global subscription portals into clear, structured user guides.
